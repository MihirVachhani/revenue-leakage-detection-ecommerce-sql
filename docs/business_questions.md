# Business Questions
## Revenue Leakage Detection in an E-Commerce Marketplace

---
# Primary Business Question

Which operational factors create the greatest revenue leakage risk within the marketplace?

---

# Theme 1: Marketplace Revenue Performance

## BQ1

What is the total Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) generated by the marketplace?

### Why It Matters

Provides the baseline measure of marketplace activity.

### KPI

GMV

### Stakeholder

Executive Leadership

---
## BQ2

How has marketplace revenue changed over time?

### Why It Matters

Identifies growth trends and periods of revenue instability.

### KPI

Monthly GMV

### Stakeholder

Leadership Team

---

## BQ3

Which product categories generate the most revenue?

### Why It Matters

Identifies key revenue-driving product segments.

### KPI

Revenue by Category

### Stakeholder

Commercial Team

---

# Theme 2: Revenue Leakage Investigation

## BQ4

What percentage of orders are cancelled?

### Why It Matters

Cancelled orders represent unrealized revenue opportunities.

### KPI

Cancellation Rate

### Stakeholder

Operations Team

---

## BQ5

What percentage of orders become unavailable?

### Why It Matters

Unavailable products indicate seller or inventory problems.

### KPI

Unavailable Rate

### Stakeholder

Seller Management Team

---

## BQ6

Which product categories contribute most to cancelled or unavailable orders?

### Why It Matters

Identifies operational weaknesses by category.

### KPI

Cancellation Exposure

### Stakeholder

Operations Team
