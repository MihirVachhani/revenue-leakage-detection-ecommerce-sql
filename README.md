## Business Problem

An e-commerce marketplace is experiencing operational inefficiencies that may be causing hidden revenue leakage through cancellations, delivery failures, fulfillment issues, and poor customer experiences.

The objective is to identify and quantify these leakage sources using SQL-based analysis.
