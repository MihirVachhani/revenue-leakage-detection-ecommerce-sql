# Exploratory Business Analysis
## Revenue Leakage Detection in an E-Commerce Marketplace

---

# Objective

Before investigating revenue leakage, it is important to understand how the marketplace operates.

This exploratory analysis establishes baseline business metrics including marketplace size, revenue generation, seller ecosystem, customer satisfaction, and fulfilment performance.

The findings serve as the foundation for later revenue leakage investigations.
